Static demo of Jazz Generation.

Solos and accompaniments were pre-generated with the project's Python engine.
Audio is synthesized in your browser from MIDI using:

- FluidSynth (libfluidsynth, compiled to WebAssembly) - LGPL-2.1, see synth/LICENSE.fluidsynth.txt
- js-synthesizer by jet - BSD-3-Clause, see synth/LICENSE.js-synthesizer.txt
- TimGM6mb.sf2 General MIDI SoundFont by Tim Brechbill - GPL-2.0
